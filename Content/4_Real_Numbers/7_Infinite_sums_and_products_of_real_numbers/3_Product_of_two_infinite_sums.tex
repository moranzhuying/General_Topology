\section{两个无限和的乘积}

未加声明时,$\left(x_{i}\right)_{i\in I}$,$\left(y_{j}\right)_{j\in J}$是$\R$的元素族.

\begin{proposition}{}{}
	设$\left(x_{i}\right)_{i\in I}$和$\left(y_{j}\right)_{j\in J}$可和,则$\left(x_{i}y_{j}\right)_{\left(i,j\right)\in I\times J}$可和,并且$\sum\limits_{\left(i,j\right)\in I\times J}x_{i}y_{j}=\sum\limits_{i\in I}x_{i}\sum\limits_{j\in J}y_{j}$.
\end{proposition}
